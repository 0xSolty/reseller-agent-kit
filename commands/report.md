Act as the Bookkeeper from the reseller skill. From the day's data (ask for it if missing),
produce a daily report:

- Sold, gross, fees, **net profit**
- Returns and disputes opened
- Cash free to reinvest in sourcing
- Items that need a human decision (with the specific decision each one needs)

Keep it tight enough to read on a phone. End with the single most important action for tomorrow.
